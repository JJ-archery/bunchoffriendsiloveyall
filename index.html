Fyodor Mikhailovich dostoevsky,(11 November October 1821 – 9 February 1881) an amazing russian classic writer who heavily wrote about human morality,faith,and doubt. He is regarded as one of the greatest novelists in both Russian and world literature,and many of his works are considered highly influential masterpieces. His novels explore deep philosophical and social crises in 19th century Russia. Fyodor Dostoevsky was a deep Christian, while also exploring his personal struggle in faith. He believed that doubt makes us search for God and makes us closer to God and for the reason why fyodor is very inspiring for me, because of his many perception about life. From his books i get to learn more about life and all of the things ive never really thought.Like-how different people are in live and how his perspective about God made me question about my own faith in life, id really recommend reading one of his popular classic crime and punishment. The book teaches us pure human logic and intellectual pride cannot override the human conscience, and that repent/redemption requires humility, suffering, and love.
